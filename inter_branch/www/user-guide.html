User Guide

Inter Branch for ERPNext

One company, several branches. This app books the obligation every cross-branch movement creates, so each branch's own P&L and balance sheet stand up on their own — without inflating consolidated turnover.

ERPNext v15 – v16
App: inter_branch
Requires: frappe + erpnext
What This App Does

The problem it solves, and the four things it gives you.

Branch A ships stock to Branch B, or does work for it. The company total is always right, but each branch's own books are wrong: A carries a cost B benefited from, and nothing in standard ERPNext records the obligation between them.

Inter Branch records it — as a Due from / Due to pair on the balance sheet, plus (for services) a recharge pair across the P&L boundary.

Inter Branch Configuration Master · one per branch Asset / liability account heads, recharge overrides, warehouse and cost-centre mappings

The Accounting Model

What gets posted, and why a service costs four legs instead of two.

Stock crossing a branch boundary — 2 legs

Stock is an asset moving. The stock GL is already written by the Stock Entry; these two legs settle who owes whom.

LegAccountRoot typeBranch tag
DrDue from receiverAssetsender
CrDue to senderLiabilityreceiver

A service crossing a branch boundary — 4 legs

A service has no asset: by the time one branch has done the work, the cost is already sunk in the performer's P&L. Two extra legs carry the cost across the boundary.

LegAccountRoot typeBranch tag
CrInter-Branch Service RecoveredIncomeperformer
DrDue from receiverAssetperformer
DrInter-Branch Service ChargedExpensereceiver
CrDue to performerLiabilityreceiver

Why not an internal Sales Invoice?

An internal sale inflates consolidated turnover by the recharge amount every time, and has to be eliminated by hand at every close. Recovered and Charged are equal and opposite by construction, so the elimination is structural.
Service Recovered is Income root-type, but it is not turnover. The app stamps custom_exclude_from_turnover on the account. Any revenue KPI, dashboard or statutory report that sums Income must filter on that flag.
Install

Two bench commands. Everything else is idempotent and re-runs on migrate.

bench get-app inter_branchFetch the app into the bench (source supplied by Enfono Technologies)
bench --site <site> install-app inter_branchInstall on the site
bench --site <site> migrateRe-applies custom fields, the Branch dimension and parent account groups
Install and migrate create: the Branch Accounting Dimension (with its branch columns on the accounting and stock child tables), the Inter Branch Settings single doc, and the inter-branch parent account groups for every enabled company. All of it is safe to re-run.
Step 1 — Inter Branch Settings

Desk → search Inter Branch Settings. One row per company, plus site-wide behaviour.

1

Add a company row

Nothing posts for a company that has no row here. Submitting an inter-branch document on an unlisted company is refused with Company Not Configured.

2

Set the Cut-over Date

Journal Entries dated before it are left alone by the auto-injector. A blank date keeps the automatic posting layer off for that company — the safe default while you configure the rest. Manually created Stock Transfers and Service Settlements still post.

3

Turn on Enforce Branch Dimension — last

Do this once every warehouse is mapped. It marks Branch mandatory for Balance Sheet and P&L accounts at the GL-posting layer, which is what makes branch statements trustworthy — and what blocks postings that would be untraceable.

Adding a Branch on a site with exactly one enabled company creates its configuration stub automatically. With more than one company, you get a reminder instead — which company a branch belongs to is a human decision.

One Branch, one Company

A Branch belongs to exactly one Company. A transfer or settlement on company Y whose branch is configured for company X is refused up front. If the same physical branch trades under two companies, create a Branch per company (Riyadh — Acme, Riyadh — Globex) and configure each separately.
Step 3 — Stock Account Rules

Two conditions a stock transfer must meet, or it is refused at submit.

1

Perpetual Inventory must be on for the company

Without it a Material Transfer writes no stock GL at all, so the companion Journal Entry would stand alone and both branches' trial balances would be out by the value of every transfer.

Where: Company → Accounts → Enable Perpetual Inventory (and its stock accounts).

Service settlements are unaffected by this.

2

Each branch's warehouse needs its own stock account

ERPNext writes a stock leg per warehouse, and only when the two warehouses resolve to different accounts. Two warehouses on one account produce a debit and a credit that merge to zero — nothing is written at all.

  • Set Warehouse → Account per branch warehouse, or
  • give each one a parent warehouse that carries its own account.

A warehouse with no account of its own falls back to its parent, then to the company's Default Inventory Account, then to any Stock account on the company — and that last fallback is what silently lands two branches on one shared account.

A transfer that fails either test is refused at submit, with a table naming the offending warehouses. That is deliberate: posting it would create a companion Journal Entry with nothing to offset it.
Inter Branch Stock Transfer

Moves stock between two branches and books the obligation that movement creates.

Draft → Submitted → Return → Cancelled
1

Create the transfer

  • Company, Posting Date — the series is IBST-.YYYY.-
  • Source and Target Warehouse — the branches are derived from them and shown read-only
  • Items — item, qty, UOM. Rate defaults to the price list when one is configured, otherwise to valuation
  • Customer (optional) — only when the target branch sells the goods onward
2

Save — validation runs

  • Both warehouses must map to a branch, and the two branches must differ
  • Both branches must belong to the document's company
  • Every row needs a distinct source and target warehouse
  • Totals and valuation rates are recalculated
3

Submit — three documents are created

  • Stock Entry (Material Transfer) for the physical move, with its GL legs re-tagged so the credit sits on the sending branch and the debit on the receiving one
  • Journal Entry — the Due from / Due to pair, valued from the Stock Entry's real valuation rates
  • Delivery Note — only when a Customer is set and the company row allows it; it ships out of the target warehouse

Pre-submit checks: perpetual inventory, postable stock accounts, and enough stock in the warehouse the goods actually leave.

4

After submit — buttons on the form

  • View → Stock Entry / Journal Entry / Delivery Note
  • Create → Return — drafts a return for whatever is still returnable
  • View → Returns — item-wise transferred / returned / remaining breakdown
  • Create Journal Entry — appears only when the obligation was not posted (usually a zero valuation at submit time). Fix the rates, then use it

Returns & Cancellation

Sending stock back, and undoing a transfer that should not have happened.

ActionWhat happens
Create → Return Drafts a new transfer with Is Return set and Return Against pointing at the original, pre-filled with the quantities still returnable. Goods move in the opposite direction; the obligation is booked the opposite way round.
Partial returns Allowed and cumulative — a return can never exceed what is left. Only one draft return per original at a time.
Returning a return Refused.
Cancel Cascades in reverse order of creation: companion Journal Entry → Delivery Note → Stock Entry, then the transfer is marked Cancelled. A submitted return can be cancelled on its own; the original's link to it is released first.
Cancel the transfer, not its Stock Entry or Journal Entry directly — the cascade only runs from the transfer.
Inter Branch Service Settlement

One branch did work for another. Series IBSS-.YYYY.-.

1

Pick the two branches

Performing Branch did the work and carries the cost today. Receiving Branch benefited and should carry it. They must differ, and both must belong to the document's company.

The recharge accounts are previewed onto the form as soon as both are picked, and frozen onto the document on save so a later default change cannot rewrite history.

2

Choose a pricing basis

  • Flat Amount — one figure in the header; item rows are informational
  • Hours × Rate — per row: hours × rate
  • Cost Plus Percentage — per row: cost × (1 + mark-up%), using the row's mark-up or the header default

The total must be greater than zero.

3

Submit — one four-leg Journal Entry

Recovered (Income, performer) · Due from receiver (Asset, performer) · Charged (Expense, receiver) · Due to performer (Liability, receiver). The link appears on the document; cancelling the settlement cancels that Journal Entry.

Automatic Postings

Three things the app does to documents you did not create through it.

Branch tagging on stock documents

Stock Entry, Stock Reconciliation, Delivery Note, Purchase Receipt, Sales Invoice, Purchase Invoice and Material Request get each row's Branch filled from the warehouse mapping, then the header branch copied into rows still blank.

The warehouse wins over a branch picked by hand — with an on-screen notice — unless the user holds a trusted role.

Direct cross-branch Stock Entries

A Material Transfer Stock Entry created outside this app gets the same companion Journal Entry on submit, and it is cancelled with the entry.

Skipped — and logged — when the entry spans several branch pairs, when perpetual inventory is off, or when the warehouses would write no stock GL. Repacks and manufacturing entries are never touched.

Multi-branch Journal Entries

A manual Journal Entry touching two branches is balanced automatically: the Due from / Due to legs are injected from the per-branch imbalance.

Three or more is ambiguous — who owes whom cannot be inferred — so the company's Bridge Branch becomes the counterparty for all the others. The entry is rejected if the bridge is not configured, or not present in the entry.

Injected legs are marked Auto-inserted Inter-Branch Leg and carry the source document, so re-saving a Journal Entry rebuilds them instead of stacking duplicates. Entries dated before the company's cut-over date are left alone.
Inter Branch Reconciliation

The one report to check before every close.

Branch × branch matrix

Rows are the branch holding the balance, columns the counterparty. Healthy state: A→B and B→A net to zero, and the Unmatched column is zero on every row. A non-zero figure is shown in red.

Filters
Company (required) From Date To Date Hide Branches With No Activity

What a non-zero Unmatched means

A manual Journal Entry was posted to one side only, a companion entry was cancelled without its source document, or a document is still mid-flight. Balances are read from the GL against the account heads on Inter Branch Configuration — renaming an account head does not drop it from the report.
